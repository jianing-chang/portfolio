One year on the feed.

I was Vice President of the Durham University Taiwanese Society from June 2022 to June 2023 and ran its Instagram for the year. I made all 44 posts from that period. I filmed and edited the videos, wrote the copy and designed every post in Canva. I used Instagram's analytics to see which content our members engaged with most, and changed what we posted to meet their needs.
